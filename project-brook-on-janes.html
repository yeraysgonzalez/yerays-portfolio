01 / Homepage direction — layered sections, confident color and a clear content hierarchy

The idea

Experience & art direction

A residential website that feels like a place with a personality—not another property listing.

The visual direction pairs deep navy, rich crimson and quiet white space with subtle contour-like textures. Oversized editorial panels, concise headlines and lifestyle imagery create a memorable rhythm while giving floor plans, amenities, neighborhood and contact information clear destinations.

03 / Responsive concept mockups inspired by the supplied mobile layouts

The visual system

Color / Type / Texture

Bold contrast. Refined details. A flexible identity across screens.

Navy establishes depth and confidence, crimson brings energy to key messages, and warm white keeps dense information readable. A mix of strong sans-serif headlines, delicate supporting type and understated directional arrows connects the desktop and mobile layouts.
